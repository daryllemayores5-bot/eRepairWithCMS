  eRepair is a college capstone platform connecting customers with laptop and mobile device repair technicians in Philippine communities.

  Search by device, repair specialization and service area. Compare professional profiles, request a repair, coordinate an appointment and follow the repair progress.

  Technicians must be approved by an administrator before appearing in the public directory. Repair estimates require customer approval before repair work begins.
