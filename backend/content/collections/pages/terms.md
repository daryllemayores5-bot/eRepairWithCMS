content: |
  eRepair helps customers discover repair technicians and coordinate repair requests. Confirm device ownership, the repair scope, estimated price, appointment location and any warranty directly with your selected technician.

  Provide accurate account and device information. Upload only information and photos you are permitted to share. Do not include passwords or unrelated personal files in repair descriptions or attachments.

  A request is subject to technician acceptance and appointment coordination. The customer approves the submitted estimate before repair work proceeds. Customers confirm handover and completion before leaving one review for a completed repair.

  Administrators may review technician profiles, suspend accounts and moderate reviews. Administrator approval of a profile is not a guarantee of a particular repair outcome.

  This educational demonstration does not process online payments. Production-specific operator, support and dispute arrangements must be published by the institution or operator before public operation.
