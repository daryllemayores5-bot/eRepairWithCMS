content: |
  eRepair uses account names, email addresses, professional profiles and repair information to connect customers and technicians and coordinate repairs.

  Approved technician profiles, approximate service areas and published customer reviews are public. Device serial numbers, repair descriptions, photos and appointment locations are available to authorized repair participants and administrators.

  Account sessions use cookies. The installable app caches static application files; it does not deliberately cache private repair or authentication API responses. Public maps request tiles from OpenStreetMap, whose providers receive ordinary connection information when the map is opened.

  This is an educational demonstration. Use sample information when demonstrating the project. The institution or operator must publish its actual contact details, retention arrangements and privacy procedures before collecting real production data.
